# The Church-Turing thesis

The claim that anything effectively calculable is computable by a Turing
machine, and equivalently by lambda calculus. It is a thesis rather than a
theorem: "effectively calculable" is an intuition about what a person with
pencil and paper could do, not a formal object.

Every model of computation proposed since has turned out to be equivalent or
weaker.
