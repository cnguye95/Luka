Grace Hopper

# Grace Hopper

Mathematician and rear admiral. Wrote the first compiler, popularized the idea that a programming language could read like English, and spent decades arguing that "we have always done it this way" is the most dangerous phrase in the language.

The moth taped into the Mark II logbook was not the origin of the word bug, which was already old, but it was a good joke.
