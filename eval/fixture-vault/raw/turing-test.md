# The imitation game

Turing's 1950 proposal, usually called the Turing test: a judge converses with a
human and a machine through text and tries to tell which is which. It sidesteps
"can machines think" by replacing it with a question you can actually run.

It is not a definition of intelligence and Turing did not offer it as one.
