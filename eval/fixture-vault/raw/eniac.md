# ENIAC

Completed in 1945 at the Moore School: eighteen thousand vacuum tubes, decimal
rather than binary, and programmed by physically rewiring it. The six women who
did that programming — Betty Holberton, Jean Bartik and their colleagues — were
doing work nobody yet had a name for.

Rewiring took days. That is the problem the stored-program idea solves.
