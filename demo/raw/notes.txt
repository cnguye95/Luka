Reading notes

The argument for compiling a wiki rather than accumulating documents is that
the links are the index. A pile of PDFs has no structure a retriever can walk;
a wiki has one, written by the same model that read the sources.

Luka takes that literally. The wikilink graph is not a visualization of the
retrieval mechanism, it is the retrieval mechanism, and the graph pane exists
so that the ranking can be inspected instead of assumed.

Plain text sources are passed through rather than converted. The only thing
compile changes here is the frontmatter block at the top.
