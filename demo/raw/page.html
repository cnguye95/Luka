Personalized PageRank in one page

PageRank scores a node by the stationary distribution of a random walker on the graph. Personalized PageRank changes exactly one thing: when the walker teleports, it lands on a chosen seed set rather than on a uniformly random node.

The update

Each iteration mixes a damped walk over the degree-normalized adjacency matrix with a teleport back to the personalization vector. Damping decides how far influence spreads from the seeds before it decays.

  • A node with no edges propagates nothing and holds teleport mass only.
  • Convergence is measured as the L1 distance between successive vectors.
  • Fixing the node order makes the whole computation reproducible.

Why it suits a wiki

The links a writer chooses are a much stronger signal than incidental word overlap, and they are already there. Nothing has to be embedded, chunked, or re-indexed when a page changes.
